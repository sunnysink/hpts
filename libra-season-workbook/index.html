Harmony and Growth

September 23 ~ October 22

Libra Season represents a new start. As a cardinal sign, this energy generates new vibrations for us to harness and use to create the changes we want in our lives.

Each astrological energy is an evolution of the one before. Virgo Season taught us to accept our imperfections and understand the value of our self-worth. Libra Season builds on that and seeks to extend it to our partnerships. Once we can accept our own imperfections, we are in a place to accept other people's imperfections too.

When we know our true self-worth, we can better stand our ground in conflict and find a peaceful resolution that preserves our integrity. We also can attract partners into our lives who reflect our self-worth and can help us on a journey of self-discovery.

This season is ultimately an opportunity to look at the relationships in your life and understand them on a new level. Which ones are serving you? Which ones are limiting you? Relationships can be complicated and messy, and they also bring you into an energetic vibration you could not get to alone.

Libra is a cardinal air sign that opens the fall season. There is a sense of slowing down and taking a moment of rest and appreciation for what the last season gave us.

There is also a cooperation with nature happening. Each of us is needed to bring in the harvest and see that it is properly stored. Libra is a sign of cooperation and balance.

Ruled by Venus, Libra wants to create beauty and luxury through relationships, balance, and creativity. Libra encourages harmonious relationships that are mutually beneficial and fair.

Libra can be considered a turning point in the year. It is the midpoint in the zodiac, when we transition from being primarily focused on our own personal growth to a more community focus. Libra teaches us of karma and that what we reap, we will sow.

The Highest Expression

The highest expression of Libra is one that balances the higher soul with the incarnated personality. It seeks fairness and right relationships by allowing all sides and voices to be heard and considered.

The Work of Libra Season

Cultivating Healthy Relationships

Five practices to build relationships that grow with you rather than around you.

Being Honest

Healthy relationships need open, honest communication. Without it, the relationship will struggle. Be mindful of how you can balance kindness and compassion with honesty. Sometimes being honest can come across as unkind.

Balancing compassion with honesty helps the relationship grow. Without compassion, the other person may become defensive. Try to see things through their eyes and remember they may not see it as you do, yet. Take your time to communicate, and try to understand their perspective.

Managing Expectations

Putting unreasonable expectations on others leads to disappointment. You will have many people in your life — all with very different roles and relationships to you. Some are your soul mates and always will be. Others may be acquaintances and never anything more. Understanding these differences helps you manage expectations and save you from being hurt down the road.

How to Grow Together

As time goes by, we and those around us change and evolve. This is good. Growing together helps maintain and improve the relationship instead of growing apart. To sustain healthy relationships, flexibility is key. As each person changes and evolves into other versions of themselves, find ways to support their growth. Talk about how you can support each other so the transition is seamless.

When a Relationship Comes to an End

Letting go of a relationship is sometimes the healthiest thing to do. Relationships sometimes just run their course, and that's natural. When they do, know that you have the freedom to walk away if that feels right for you. You don't need to justify your decision, but it's compassionate to let them know that you are doing so.

Giving Your Appreciation

We all love to feel appreciated, so don't forget to tell the people in your life every day how grateful you are for the ways they make your life happier. Set this as an intention. It's easily overlooked.

The Turning of the Wheel

Fall Equinox

SEPTEMBER 22

The Balance of Light and Dark

The doorway into fall — day and night in equal measure

Fall brings our attention inward to appreciate what is about to be harvested, internally and externally. The crops reach their full potential and nature sheds her leaves. It is a time of detoxification. Plants let go of what they no longer need, animals begin to hibernate, and we spend more time indoors as the sun sets earlier and earlier.

The cycle begins again in spring and continues, showing us that nothing is permanent in nature, but many things are predictable.

Full Moon in Aries

Energies of Libra + Aries

Aries brings us the opportunity to burn away anything blocking our path forward. This Full Moon is a time to align with your inner power and feel your courage to overcome any obstacle. It is also a time to align with your passions.

Aries guides us on the mission to find our soul's purpose. Finding this path takes commitment, motivation, and fearlessness. Our life path is not always an easy journey. This Full Moon is here to help you walk a little farther on it with more confidence than you've ever felt before.

This Aries Full Moon is the time to look at how the boundaries in your relationships may be weak. If anger often arises in your relationships, ask yourself if there are things you need to say that you may be avoiding. Ask yourself why you are avoiding them. Then think of how you can calmly and constructively state your needs in a way that reinforces your boundaries and dissipates your frustration.
