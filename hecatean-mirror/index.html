Before you read

How to Read

Hecate is present here as a companion of thresholds, keys, and crossroads. You decide what a card means in your circumstances.

  1. Begin with an open question. Try “What am I ready to see about this choice?” or “What support would help me act with integrity?”
  2. Hold the torch to draw one card, or lay one of the three spreads.
  3. Notice your first response to the title and symbol. What stands out? What feeling or memory arrives?
  4. Read the entry and choose one mirror question. The guide offers possibilities; your context decides what fits.
  5. Close with one grounded step. Resting, gathering information, and asking for support are all steps.

Reversed cards

Turn on reversed cards beside the torch if you read them. Use the same entry and ask where its theme is muted, resisted, overextended, or ready to return. Reading every card upright works just as well.

At your own pace

Set a question down whenever it feels like too much. Try a shorter reading, orient to the room, or come back another day. This deck is a tool for reflection. When you need care, reach out to the people you trust and to qualified professionals.

A one card daily practice

Ask, “What deserves my attention today?” Draw a card, write one sentence about its symbol, and choose one mirror question. In the evening, note where the theme appeared.
