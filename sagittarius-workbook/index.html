Creating Joy and Spontaneity

Sagittarius season encourages us to enjoy life, process past events and be hopeful for what's next. This season's reminder is that you have the power to create joy, optimism, and meaning in your life. Known as the student, philosopher, and wanderer, Sagittarius reminds us that everyone has something to teach us. We have never learned all there is to learn here in this world!

Sagittarius season inspires visions of travel and adventure. You may have the urge to experience something new, something unique, or something that will change your perceptions and points of view. Sagittarius teaches us that the journey matters most.

Sagittarius season is a great time to view all events as experiences that help us evolve. Let go of the idea of negative and positive occurrences, and see all situations as teachers meant to expand us and lead us on this journey of life.

The mutable fire sign Sagittarius begins its season at the end of autumn, as we move into the cold days that usher in winter. We must have a vision of what is to come to ensure proper planning and distribution of resources to sustain us through winter. Sagittarius is the visionary who shows the way to higher understanding through its storing of knowledge and its courageous, independent exploration of higher truth. Sagittarius has a passion for philosophy, teaching, travel, religion, and belief.

Sagittarius's ruling planet is Jupiter, which endows it with a deep sense of faith, a drive for higher perspective, and adventure. The mutable fire nature, combined with Jupiter's influence, creates a passionate and progressive personality that seeks to understand the world and give that understanding back with greater clarity.

It can be difficult to keep Sagittarius in place for very long, as it always feels the call for adventure and the exploration of knowledge and experience. Sagittarius is the sign of the hunter and brings the energy of learning and wisdom.

The Highest Expression

The highest expression of Sagittarius is to bring a higher understanding through an elevated awareness of the self's spiritual nature. In this way Sagittarius learns to turn the archer's arrow inward and bring that inner evolution into illumination, by integrating faith with knowledge and translating it into information that will serve humanity.

The Work of Sagittarius Season

Aligning with Your Dreams

Six steps for calling in what you want this season.

Step 1

Own Your Desires

It's time to really define your dreams! What exactly do you want? State your clear desires to yourself and others, and notice if any doubts come up. Do you feel worthy of this dream? Know you ARE worthy, and tell the Universe that you are ready to have them in your life!

✓ Saved
Step 2

Explore Your Desires

If there was nothing standing in the way, what would you like to BE, DO, or HAVE? Write a desire list at least once per day. You can write the same desires as the day before, and as you keep going day by day you will think of even more things that you want! Your daily desire list lives on the Desires & Affirmations page.

Step 3

Let Go of Resistance

Resistance is natural. Dreams require change, and change can be scary. As you write your desire list, tune into your body. Does anything feel tense? Are there any thoughts or limiting beliefs coming up? If so, focus on deep breaths of acceptance. Have a conversation with your fears and doubts and ask what they are about. Learn from them, and let yourself know you are ready for your desires to become manifest!

✓ Saved
Step 4

List Out Small Steps

What's something you can do to manifest your dreams? Close your eyes and ask your higher self, "What is one step I can take today to get me closer to my dream?" Complete these small tasks with firm belief that you have control of your destiny and are co-creating with the Universe!

Step 5

Become the Future You

What would you be thinking, feeling, and doing when you reach these goals? What would you be saying to others? Embody this to truly become this version of you!

✓ Saved
Step 6

Believe This Can Be Your Reality

Feel into your dreams. Believe that this can occur for you! Trust the process and practice gratitude. Be grateful even for the little things! Be grateful for signs, and even for yourself for believing in the power of your dream!

Full Moon in Gemini

Energies of Sagittarius + Gemini

TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 24

A Festive Moon

Open your heart, mind and energy to the new vibrations coming in.

During this Full Moon, open up your heart, mind and energy to the new vibrations that bring in change over the next year. This Full Moon is the perfect time to be with friends and family and reflect on the year as it passes.

Gemini is an air sign ruled by Mercury, and it's the most social sign of the zodiac, inspiring us to exchange energy with those around us. The Gemini Full Moon is a festive moon, a time to release any emotional turbulence from the year so that new, fresh and vibrant energies can enter in the new year.

Gemini is the sign of the ever-curious Twins. This energy represents the duality of the Universe that is present within all of us. It is a mirror to our own changing energy and shows us how paradoxical life can become.

As you work with this Full Moon, feel where your energy has become divided. Where is your mind saying one thing, but your heart is saying another? Where are you feeling indecisive or insecure in your decisions? This Full Moon is a time to communicate with both yourself and others.
