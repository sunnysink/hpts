The Practice

How to pathwork with an archetype

Pathworking is a meditative journey into the world of a card. You step into its energy, meet the archetype as a presence, and let its wisdom surface. Five steps carry you in and back out again.

1

Choose an archetype

Select a Major Arcana card that resonates with you or reflects a current phase in your life. Ask whether its energy is a lesson, a strength, or a challenge.

2

Create a sacred space

Find a quiet, comfortable place where you won't be disturbed. Light a candle, burn incense, or play soft music. Place the card in front of you as a focal point.

3

Begin the visualization

Close your eyes and breathe. Let your body relax, then picture yourself stepping into the world of the card. What do you see? What do you feel? Imagine the archetype there with you.

4

Engage with the archetype

Notice its surroundings, actions, and presence. Ask what it wants to teach you, what it knows about your path, how you can carry its energy. Then listen for whatever comes.

5

Reflect and integrate

Thank the archetype and picture yourself returning to your own world. Journal the insights, emotions, and messages, then choose one way to live its lesson today.
