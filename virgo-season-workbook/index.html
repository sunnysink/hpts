From the fires of Leo Season, we enter the grounding energy of Virgo. Each zodiac sign brings us an array of vibrations and frequencies to work with in our daily lives.

It does not matter if you do not have Virgo predominantly in your chart. You can still work with this energy to access and understand the parts of your personality that correlate with the frequency of Virgo. We all carry these frequencies within our energetic fields.

Virgo is the sign of the goddess. This divinely feminine energy can teach us many things about our power to heal, grow, and align with our intuition. All beings carry this energy, and it does not matter what gender you were assigned at birth—you still hold the feminine energy of Virgo and can work with her gifts.

This four-week Sun Season is the time to look at and develop these frequencies so they work for us instead of against us. Grab a journal and pen and let's get started.

Virgo is a mutable earth sign that begins at the end of summer. This is the time when we begin to harvest the season's abundance. It's a time of sorting, organizing, and preparing for the cooler months to come.

Virgo is an energy of practical application, concentrating on how we work and organize our lives. Virgo is also connected to the body, encouraging attention to the body for optimal health and integration.

Virgo is ruled by Mercury, giving it a mental and communicative nature. As an earth sign, this mental energy is grounded, structured, and systematic. Because it's also mutable, there is a flexible nature for facing change that the other earth signs don't express. This combo leads to practical mental energy that knows how to refine what we have taken time to create in our physical lives.

Virgo unites the body and the mind and allows for conscious evolution through practical application that can see the highest spiritual expression of ourselves.

The Highest Expression

Virgo's true power is embodied in the harvest and reaping of seeds. Virgo encourages you to allow wisdom to move through you by coming from a place of centered awareness within. The highest expression of Virgo is using its wisdom, grounding energy, and practicality in service of all. Virgo is also a sign of healing.

Realizing Your Self-Worth

You Are Worthy

Understand that you are worthy of Love, Acceptance, and Appreciation.

Recognize Your Worth

We often recognize when the people around us aren't showing their love, acceptance, or appreciation in the way that we would like. But when you stop and reflect on your own love, acceptance, and appreciation of yourself, you may recognize that the lack of appreciation may be coming from how you view yourself.

It's time to declare your sense of worthiness. Know that you are vital to this world and that your energy brings something irreplaceable to the collective.

You Don't Have to Earn It

You may have been taught that your worthiness is dependent on something. For me, it was always how I looked and what I provided for others. But just know that this can come in many forms.

You may be placing conditions on your worthiness based on how busy you are... how much money you have... your sense of success... how big your family is. There are so many conditions we place on our worthiness. During Virgo season, know that you don't have to do anything to "earn" your unique place in this world.

Get to Know Yourself

Our worth comes from knowing ourselves. We need to understand what makes us happy, what we are naturally good at, and how we make others feel. These things help us know our worth.

Throughout Virgo Season, journal about who you are and what you like about yourself. This is not a list of accomplishments but rather a list of characteristics that define who you are. What do you offer the people around you? How are you unique? How do you overcome adversity? Reflect upon these questions each day and feel into your true essence.

Once and For All, Release Comparison

Let go of comparison for good. You have your own unique DNA, your own unique fingerprint, and you are uniquely you as no one else can be. You are also connected to all other living energy, not just on this planet, but in the universe.

Celebrate the divinity inside of you. Embody the Goddess energy of Virgo season and understand that everyone else has that same divine energy.

Full Moon in Pisces

Energies of Virgo + Pisces

The Pisces Full Moon is a magical time with the potential for great healing and release. Pisces represents the vastness of the Universe and teaches us the interconnectedness of all beings.

Symbolized by the fish and ruled by Neptune, this sign inspires us to expand our dreams and feel our internal mysticism. This Full Moon is a time to embrace your most spiritual self and open your consciousness to the possibilities of the cosmos.

The Pisces Full Moon is a time to honor your intuition and the flow of your life. It's an opportunity to feel the true subtleties of your energy and know that magic lies in the depths of your being.
