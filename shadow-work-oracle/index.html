How to work with this deck

For yourself

Pull a Shadow card and write for ten minutes without editing. Close with a Lantern card and say it out loud. Your reflections save to your journal in this browser.

With a client

Switch to A client and every Shadow card shows the question to ask in session. Draw together on a call, then let the Lantern card be the line they take home.

The Candle Path

A three-card spread. The Root names what is buried, the Moon names what is ready to be seen, and the Lantern gives you the action or belief to carry forward.

Shadow work stirs things up. Go at your own pace, and refer a client to a licensed therapist when they need more support than coaching holds.
