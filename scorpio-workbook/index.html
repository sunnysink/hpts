Unleash Your Inner Power Through Shadow Work

October 23 ~ November 21

Scorpio Season is a magical time that brings us the opportunity to face our deepest shadows, accept them, and grow with them instead of away from them. This season teaches us how to become our own shamans, alchemists, and psychologists as we travel to the deepest layers of our beings.

As the Sun sits in the energy of Scorpio, it brings us the courage to face our whole selves and a willingness to face our hidden truths. Scorpio reveals, and those revelations can be life-changing. Scorpio inspires us to do the work on ourselves needed for personal growth and evolution.

This season asks us to see and accept truths about ourselves we would rather ignore. Scorpio teaches us that we are strong, resilient, and intuitive. We can face anything about ourselves or others, and we can survive even our hardest days.

As we begin this new season, take the calmness and balance you found in Libra and hold on to it. It is from this place of inner harmony that you can truly begin the work Scorpio Season offers. Return to your breath and your body when you need to, and remember that you have the power to return to peace, no matter how turbulent the waters around you become.

The fixed water sign Scorpio begins its season mid autumn, as the days get shorter and colder. The trees and foliage fade and die as they release their leaves back to the earth.

In the same way, Scorpio season takes us into our inner depths and draws us deep within to explore what we need to understand and release about the past season, as we move toward the end of the year and the beginning of the next. Scorpio is a deeply emotional and intimate sign that can get to the bottom of things and understand where transformation needs to take place for higher expression.

Scorpio is a seeker of truth and revealer of secrets. A fixed sign, Scorpio represents a very determined and at times insatiable aspect of the water element. Scorpio is deep, intriguing, passionate, and oftentimes a bit wild. Scorpio is also easily hurt and can shut down toward those it feels have done it wrong. It can also be deeply honest and seeks to bring out the truth about situations.

Mars and Pluto rule Scorpio. Mars takes us toward the light of the Sun with a masculine force of forward motion and confidence. Pluto's transformative force takes us deep into the underworld aspects of ourselves.

Scorpio teaches us the value of transformation through the ego death. When you allow old paradigms and ways of being to die within, you can be reborn into a newer and more conscious version of yourself.

The Highest Expression

Scorpio's highest goal is to transform the lower aspects of the personality into the higher aspects of the soul, creating an integrated being.

The Work of Scorpio Season

Tips for Working with Your Shadows

Five ways in, for facing your hidden shadows and growing with them.

Own & Accept Your Shadows

Owning and accepting our shadows helps us evolve and grow. When we leave them unacknowledged, they often interrupt our daily lives and relationships and get in the way of our dreams, our wants, and our needs. Live true, authentic, and with acceptance of yourself.

Look at Your Dreams

Our shadows may present themselves in our dreams as we sleep. We don't always remember them, so it helps to keep a dream journal. Recall your dream and write down what happens and how you feel in it. Are there recurring patterns? What are they telling you? Do they reveal anything about yourself? The Dream Journal page in this handbook is ready for you.

Judgements

How we judge others is often a mirror of what we dislike about ourselves. It's hard to accept, and perhaps that is why shadow work can be a difficult process. When someone triggers you, embrace the chance to work through the shadow that has shown up. Have a silent conversation with them: imagine talking to them, ask them questions, and wait to see what response comes to you. What is being reflected back to you? What did you learn about yourself?

Triggers

Triggers are the emotions we feel when a particular situation or experience leaves us feeling sad, angry, or anxious. A trigger could also be an event, a person, or even a location. Working out your triggers will lead you to your shadows. Look for them when an emotion or physical sensation feels difficult or just "not right."

When you are faced with your shadow, remember that your shadow doesn't belong to the present situation. The present is simply triggering your shadow so that it can be noticed.

Feel Your Emotions

Allow yourself to really feel your emotions. It's tempting to send them away, because they are often painful and can bring shame with them. Have compassion for yourself. You are human, with many emotions to work through each day. Knowing your emotions and acknowledging them will help you transform them.

Exercise, Part 3 of 3

Releasing the Limiting Belief

Step 1

Journal around the belief

  • When is the first time you remember believing this?
  • What other situations confirmed this belief for you?
  • How did it make you feel?
  • What did this belief make you believe about yourself?
✓ Saved
Step 2

Let go of your belief

Lead with your intuition.

Create a comfortable environment. Candles, smudge, an altar, soothing music.

Feel the feelings associated with the belief. Read back your journal entry, bring up the painful memory, let yourself feel that feeling.

Release the feeling and the belief. Burn your journal entry, bury it, or return it to the earth in another way.

Step 3

Call in the new belief and feelings

Immediately after releasing, choose the new belief and feel the feelings of it. Tune into it with all your senses.

Fill yourself up with all these feelings, thoughts, and ideas.

The Turning of the Wheel

Welcome Samhain

OCTOBER 31 ~ NOVEMBER 1

Summer's End

A festival of the dead, and for many, the spiritual new year

As October turns to November, thousands of Witches, Wiccans, Druids, and other Pagans across America, Canada, Europe, and elsewhere observe the sacred time of Samhain. Samhain is a festival of the Dead. Meaning "Summer's End" and pronounced saah-win or saa-ween, Samhain celebrates the end of the harvest and the start of the coldest half of the year.

For many practitioners, myself included, Samhain is also the beginning of the spiritual new year.

Full Moon in Taurus

Energies of Scorpio + Taurus

As the Full Moon rises in Taurus, feel it soften you. Feel it hold you and become a container for the immense emotions stirred by Scorpio. Feel into the comfort of the present moment, and let the Taurus energy ease any emotions brought to the surface this season.

This is a time to pause, connect with your body, connect with nature, and find stillness within. It's a time to reflect on everything you've learned so far in your journey and be with yourself in this moment. Taurus brings in peaceful vibrations that let you process the work you're doing and the growth you're experiencing.

Taurus encourages us to follow our instinct and trust it. If we are unhappy in a relationship, it's time to leave. If we want to take a trip, it's time to book it. If we feel overwhelmed, it's time to slow down. No discussion about any of it, just straightforward answers free of drama. Feel this simplicity take over the Full Moon and let it bring you some peace.
